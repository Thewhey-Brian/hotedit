How it works

The pipeline, and the objective it is trained with.

flowchart LR
  S["station history
14 days"] --> E["encoder"]
  W["weather maps"] --> E
  E --> B["bridge sampler"]
  B --> F["50 forecast
trajectories"]
  F --> C["calibration"]
  C --> O["forecast + interval"]

Each forecast starts from today's reading and is bridged to tomorrow's, instead of being generated from noise:

$$x_t=(1-t)\,x_{\text{today}}+t\,x_{\text{tomorrow}}+\sigma\sqrt{t(1-t)}\,\varepsilon,\qquad \sigma=0.2$$

The loss combines the flow term with a calibration penalty weighted by \(\lambda=0.1\):

$$\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{\text{flow}}+\lambda\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{calib}}$$
