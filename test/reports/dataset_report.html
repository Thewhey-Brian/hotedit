The corpus combines reviews from eleven public datasets. We removed exact and near duplicates, dropped reviews shorter than five words and balanced the star ratings within each language.

Splits are made by product, not by review, so no product appears in both train and test. This avoids leaking product-specific vocabulary into evaluation.

Labels follow the star rating: 1–2 stars are negative, 3 is neutral and 4–5 are positive.
